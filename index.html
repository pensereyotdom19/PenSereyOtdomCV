Software Engineer · May 2022 – Present

I develop core services for suitebook, a hotel management platform: bookings, payments and refunds, kiosk and QR checkout, account settlement, and channel manager integrations. The stack is Python with Django REST Framework, React.js, and MySQL.

Three pieces of work I’d point to

  1. Refunds as offsetting entries

    I designed refund handling so a refund is recorded as a new negative payment entry. The original payment record is never changed. That keeps the payment ledger auditable and keeps settlement totals consistent across properties.

  2. Per-property kiosk configuration

    Buildings needed different kiosk search behavior. I built the configuration as bitmask flags per property, so a building can customize its behavior without a schema change or a redeploy.

  3. Channel manager sync failures

    Inventory and rate sync with the channel manager (site controller) was failing for some properties. I traced it to mismatched OTA account IDs and room type codes, and resolving those restored the sync.
